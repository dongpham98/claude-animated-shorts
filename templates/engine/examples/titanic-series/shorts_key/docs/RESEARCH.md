Facts (2+ sources each)
1. David Blair was Titanic's Second Officer and lost his place in a last-minute shuffle of officers; Lightoller became Second Officer [ET-Glasses], [IrishPost], [Courier], [TO].
2. His postcard: "I shall have to step out to make room for (the) chief officer of the Olympic" and "I feel very disappointed..." [IrishPost]; daughter's letter quotes "I feel very disappointed I am not to make the first voyage" [TO]. Both contain "I feel very disappointed". (Olympic = Titanic's sister ship.)
3. He left with a key in his pocket ("in the rush to pack his belongings", daughter's letter) [TO], [IrishPost], [ET-Key].
4. Tag: brass, "Crows Nest Telephone Key" (one side "R.M.S. Titanic") [ET-Key], [TO daughter's letter]. What it opened is disputed: telephone box or binocular locker [ET-Key].
5. Lookouts had binoculars Belfast to Southampton, none after: Fleet "We had a pair from Belfast to Southampton... none from Southampton to New York" [US-Fleet], [ET-Glasses].
6. Lookout Hogg: Blair told him to lock the binoculars up in Blair's cabin (BI Q17501) [ET-Glasses], [ET-Key].
7. Lookouts asked Lightoller for glasses; Lightoller's own account: he told Symons "There are no glasses for you" (BI 14485) [ET-Glasses]; Symons: "Symons, there are none" [ET-Glasses]; Fleet: "they said there was none for us" [US-Fleet].
8. Fleet: "We had nothing at all, only our own eyes"; "We could have seen it a bit sooner." "How much sooner?" "Well, enough to get out of the way." [US-Fleet], recap FACTCHECK [ET-Acquit10].
9. Fleet sighted the berg ~11:39 p.m., 14 April 1912 (recap/ep02 research).
10. Binoculars were used to examine an object already seen by eye; Lightoller said ice is seen first with the naked eye [Maltin], [ET-Glasses / recap FACTCHECK N16].
11. Key sold at auction in London, 22 Sept 2007, for £90,000 [Courier], [IrishPost], [TO]. Blair's daughter Nancy had given it to the Sailors' Society; the sale funds the society's education work [Courier], [IrishPost], [TO].
