# FACTCHECK: The Real Titanic in 3 Minutes (script v1, 2026-11-08)

Checked 2026-11-08 against the US Senate and British inquiry transcripts (titanicinquiry.org), Encyclopedia Titanica, Wikipedia (and the sources it cites), Royal Museums Greenwich, Library of Congress, HM Coastguard and the USCG / International Ice Patrol pages.
Verdicts: OK = keep as is; SOFTEN = defensible but stated too firmly or too precisely; FIX = wrong or misleading.
