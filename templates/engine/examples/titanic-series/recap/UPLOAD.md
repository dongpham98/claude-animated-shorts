## Description
Titanic didn't go down because of one big mistake. Five tiny things had to go wrong, and every one of them really happened: a missing pair of binoculars, a radio buried in passengers' messages, a few seconds, a law from 1894, and a ship that was asleep just miles away.

Animated with the real people of the Titanic: Captain Edward Smith, Thomas Andrews, Wallace Hartley, Margaret Brown, lookout Fred Fleet, radio operator Jack Phillips, First Officer William Murdoch and Cyril Evans of the Californian. Every fact was checked against the 1912 US and British inquiries. Character lines in speech bubbles are either real recorded words (marked) or clearly playful.
