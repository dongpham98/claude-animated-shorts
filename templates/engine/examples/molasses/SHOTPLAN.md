# Short 01 — Shot plan (30 fps, 1080x1920, TOTAL 1849 f = 61.6 s)
Frames come from voice/words.json (word start × 30). Skill rules: every visual reaction lands 0-3 f BEFORE its word; every hold creeps (0.15-0.35 %/f push-in, 0.6 %/f on punch words); big jumps are cuts, otherwise 18-40 f smootherstep glides; impacts = flip kicks decaying over 7 f; rolls 4-5°; lens blur on close-ups/mediums only; foreground props in ~1/3 of shots.

## Sets (built once, reused)
- **A. Commercial Street / waterfront (main set):** cobbled street, 3-4 storey brick North End houses, an elevated railway on iron columns along the street, wharf and harbor water on one side, the TANK (cylinder 15 m tall × 27 m wide, riveted steel plates) at the end of the street, firehouse (2-storey, bell tower), a parked truck. Light: cold January day (day preset, cooler sky), snow patches.
- **B. Kitchen insert (analogy):** small 1910s kitchen corner: icebox/fridge, table, stove, honey jar.
- **C. Office insert (rule):** drafting table with blueprints, seal stamp, stacks of court papers.

## Cast (our own characters, bean-head style)
- Neighbor kid with a bucket, a granny with a pot, a dock worker; painter on a ladder (company man, bowler hat); townsfolk crowd (runners); rescuers in boots; an engineer; a modern-day local (ending).
